Questions

The essentials, answered.

Can I choose when my salon appears in search?

Yes. Your public profile appears only when you choose to publish it, and you can unpublish it at any time.

Do my clients need an app or an account?

No. They book from your public profile in their browser with a name and phone number.

Is GlowFlow available in more than one language?

The interface supports French, English and Spanish. Your salon's country and a visitor's language are separate choices.

How do I learn about pricing and payment methods?

Contact our team for current terms. Payment options depend on your country and salon configuration.
